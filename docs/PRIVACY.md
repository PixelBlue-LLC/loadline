# Privacy Notice (template draft)

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. This is a template-based draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer._

**Short version: we do not collect your data.**

- **Holdings, cash, settings, checklist notes and API keys** are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to PixelBlue LLC.
- **Data requests** go directly from your browser to the providers you configure (for example Finnhub for quotes and news, Anthropic for AI). Those providers receive your API key and the symbols or text needed for each request, under their own privacy policies.
- **AI questions** send a summary of your holdings for that day (symbols, weights, daily moves, related headlines) to Anthropic with your own key, only when you press a button or send a question.
- **No analytics, no cookies, no tracking** are included in the free edition.
- **Hosting**: the web app is served as static files (GitHub Pages). GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own policy.
- **Deleting your data**: use Settings → "清除全部" (clear all), or clear your browser's site data.
